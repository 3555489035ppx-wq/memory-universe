# Memory Universe Product Story

## The problem

Traditional albums preserve files but flatten the experience of remembering. A user still has to do the emotional and narrative organization manually.

## The product response

Memory Universe turns memories into a space with multiple entrances: time, people, place, emotion, music, and story chapters. The user can move closer to a single memory, step back to see relationships, or replay a theme as a continuous experience.

## Why 3D

3D is used to express proximity, grouping, movement, and rhythm. It is not decoration; it provides a spatial language for relationships that a flat list cannot express well.

## Why local-first

Personal memories are sensitive. The product starts with browser-local storage and a built-in Demo so the core experience works without login or cloud upload.

## Current product boundary

High-school memory is the complete public template. Other templates are intentionally marked Coming Soon rather than represented by empty pages. Online AI organization is specified as a future, auditable flow and is not claimed as live today.
